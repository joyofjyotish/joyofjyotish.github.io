Rasi & Bhava Karakatwas (Saravali)

Sign & house significations. Natural chart shown below (Mesha Lagna).

North Indian Diamond Chart
(Houses fixed • Signs rotate)

House 1 is always the top diamond. Signs move according to the Lagna. Counting is anti-clockwise.

South Indian Grid Chart
(Signs fixed • Houses rotate)

Signs never move (Pisces always top-left). Find the Lagna sign and count houses clockwise from it.

East Indian (Bengali / Surya Chakra)
(Signs fixed • Aries at top • Anti-clockwise)

Signs are fixed (Mesha always at the top). Count houses anti-clockwise from the Lagna sign. Common in Bengal, Odisha & the East.
